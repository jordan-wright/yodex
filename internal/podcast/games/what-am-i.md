# What Am I?

Goal: Solve a topic-connected mystery using four increasingly helpful hints.

How it works:
- Choose one specific answer that is directly related to today's topic.
- Give exactly 4 numbered hints, one at a time.
- After each hint, ask: "What am I?" followed by one `[short pause]`.
- Reveal the answer only after the fourth listener pause.
- Explain how the four hints fit the answer in one or two clear sentences.

Guidelines:
- Pick an answer that is interesting but not obvious from the first hint. Do not use a broad category such as "an animal," "a planet," or "a plant" when a more specific answer fits.
- Every hint must be accurate and related to the same answer.
- Make the hints progressively easier:
  - Hint 1 gives a broad clue about where it belongs, what it does, or why it matters.
  - Hint 2 gives a distinguishing feature or behavior.
  - Hint 3 adds a less familiar fact that rewards careful thinking.
  - Hint 4 makes the answer reasonably guessable without saying its name.
- Do not use the answer or an obvious part of its name in any hint.
- Avoid clues that could reasonably describe many different answers after the fourth hint.
- Keep each hint to one or two short sentences.
- Use this fixed structure:
  - "Hint 1: ... What am I? [short pause]"
  - "Hint 2: ... What am I? [short pause]"
  - "Hint 3: ... What am I? [short pause]"
  - "Hint 4: ... What am I? [short pause]"
  - "The answer is ...!"
- After revealing the answer, briefly celebrate the listener's thinking whether or not they guessed it.
